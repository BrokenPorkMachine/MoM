# Privacy and accessibility controls

Guest exchange files, messages, clipboard offers, pairing secrets, diagnostics, and VM backups are user-controlled data. The product does not upload them. Users may delete exchange items, rotate or remove pairing keys, export/delete diagnostics, and delete backups; VM backups are retained only at the administrator-selected destination.

Support exports redact user-home paths and secret filenames. Pairing keys are never included in diagnostics or exports. The remaining release gate is manual VoiceOver and keyboard-only validation of every VM, storage, daemon, and diagnostics action on a signed build, including focus order, labels, errors, and confirmation dialogs.
